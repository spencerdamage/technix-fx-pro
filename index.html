Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How are the example fee tiers calculated?

The deposit preview uses a sample 5% fee and the withdrawal preview a sample 2% fee. These are illustrative values only and do not represent actual platform pricing.

What currency conversion rate is used?

The currency selector uses a static illustrative conversion rate of KSH 129.50 per USD. It is not a live or guaranteed FX quote.

When do deposits and withdrawals settle?

The M-Pesa STK backend scaffold is disabled by default and does not credit a trading balance. Withdrawal processing is not integrated. No payment should be enabled until ledger, reconciliation, and support controls are ready.

Are the prices and trading activity live?

No. Prices, candlesticks, order-book depth, and trade activity are generated for this interactive preview. They should not be used for investment decisions.
